I am a fourth-year PhD candidate in Economics at the University of Maryland, College Park. I am currently working on projects in public finance and urban economics.

Prior to starting my PhD, I received a B.A. in Economics and Government from Dartmouth College and spent two years as an analyst at Cornerstone Research, where I supported economics and finance experts in commercial litigation matters and antitrust investigations. I also have experience as a consultant at the World Bank, where I collaborated with the DaTax lab, which studies taxes, firms, and jobs in developing countries using micro data.
